CTA styles for BlueStaffyUK pages

Eight button styles, painted from the site's own CSS (src/styles/cta.css) in the site's gold, ink and pill shape, which never change. No two buttons on a page may share a style or say the same thing. From now on each page board offers three options per button, and you pick one.

solidlink
Send my enquiry

The signature pill. The one plain button on a page, often the enquiry form's.

arrowlink
See the puppies available now

Goes somewhere else on the site: the litter, a puppy's page. The arrow steps forward on hover.

downlink
Ask which puppy suits your home

Jumps down to the enquiry form on this page.

chipform submit
Send my questions to Lisa

Writing to us: an envelope chip before the words. Shown here as a form submit.

sublink
Ask us to book your visitCollection in Carlisle or UK delivery

Two lines: the button, then one fact read from data/settings.json, never invented.

taglink
Tell us when you could visitCarlisle

The button, a dashed seam and a short tag read from data/settings.json.

capslink
Ask us for the full terms

Small capitals at the end of a brass rule: a quiet sign-off to a long section.

widelink
Tell us about your household

The full width of its column: the end of a card or a narrow section.
