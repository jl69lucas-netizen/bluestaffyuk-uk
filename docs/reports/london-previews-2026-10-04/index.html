1 · Mid-page call to action

Roughly 5,000 words separate the hero button from the form today. The board already plans four enquiry lines (links plan M2, "about every 600 words"), at the ends of the deposit, litter, paperwork and temperament chapters. On the page they show as underlined text, so nobody sees them as calls to action.

CTA-1 · The four existing enquiry lines become brass pills Recommended

Why. No words are added and no links are added. The pill wraps the board's own #enquiry anchors, so working rule 6 (a redesign adds or removes no content) and rule 12 (every link on the board) both hold, and rules/design.md rule 3 makes every CTA the --color-cta pill. The page then has a button at four points spread through the long middle. Trade-off. These anchors were written as sentence openers. On a phone the pill wraps to two lines, and the rest of the sentence ("…, and tell us which puppy caught your eye.") starts with a comma under the pill. Rewording would avoid that, but rewording is a board change. Gate effect: no new defect at 375 or 1280 (defect count 9 → 9 and 11 → 11), and every pill is at least 44px tall.
