# Trojanstaff UK — competitor intel

- Root domain: trojanstaffuk.com · tier 1 · analysed 2026-09-23
- Homepage gate: passed (status 200; final URL stays on trojanstaffuk.com)
- Fetched: 1 map (41 URLs) + 6 scrapes — homepage (markdown + raw HTML), puppies page, buying-process page, breed history page, contact page, one health post. Playwright: the 375px homepage check.

## Trust
The homepage prints a breeder licence number but not the council that issued it. It names Kennel Club registration, several DNA tests (L-2-HGA, HC-HSF4, PHPV/PPSC, hip scoring) and BVA eye screening, and mentions vet health checks for every puppy. It says it has bred for over a decade and gives Basingstoke as its base. A phone number and an email address are shown (email as a mailto link in the raw HTML). Reviews appear only as two screenshot images of Google reviews, so no review text is counted (0).

## Content
Homepage: 1,120 words by script, 15 H2s — long, stacked sections on stud dogs, bloodlines, puppies and ethics. The map lists 41 URLs. H2 counts on the other pages are low (2–6); most copy sits in unstyled paragraphs rather than headed sections.

## Keywords
17 phrases by the run rule. The core ones are the generic buying terms (staffy puppies for sale, staffordshire bull terrier puppies for sale, blue staffy puppies) plus breeder terms (staffy breeders, staffordshire bull terrier breeders). Two runs that passed through the kennel's own brand word were cut before the name.

The sampled health-and-wellbeing post runs 1,147 words. Posting frequency: NOT FETCHED — post URLs carry no dates and only one post was fetched.

## Visual

## Schema

## Cities
Birmingham only — named in the breed-history text, not as a service area. No city pages.

## Conversion
Buyers are asked to phone, email, fill a single-page contact form (with puppy sex and colour pickers), join the waiting list, visit to choose a puppy at four weeks, and pay a non-refundable deposit online through a third-party pet marketplace; the balance is paid at collection (8–9 weeks). No prices are printed. The only urgency is the waiting list and a line that litters are limited.

## Technical
At 375px the homepage's content is 1,179px wide, so it scrolls sideways: `mobile_layout_ok` false (the check used a desktop browser at phone width; the site builder may serve a different layout to phone user agents). Lighthouse: NOT FETCHED — no Lighthouse run in this pilot.

## Key insight
Trojanstaff wins on proof rather than pages: licence number, named DNA tests and champion lines up front, and a clear written buying journey. It has no city pages, no prices and a homepage that overflows a phone screen — BSUK can match the proof and beat it on local pages and mobile.
