## Rulings to apply

- **q01:** the pages name the tests (L-2-HGA, HC-HSF4, eye and elbow screening) and never state a result. The "tested clear" / "certified clear" lines and "will not be genetically affected" are removed or reworded to name the test only (Known Issue 98).
- **q02:** the guarantee covers health issues and birth defects within two years of ownership. The page wording, with the breeder's spelling corrected, is "covers health issues and birth defects for two years from the day your puppy comes home". It goes in `data/settings.json`. "Within two years of ownership" is read as starting when the owner takes the puppy.
- **q03:** a live video call with the puppy and its mother is offered on request, so the scam checklist may list it as a sign we pass.
- **q04:** the deposit is paid by bank transfer. The scam advice must not flag every bank transfer as a red flag. It flags a transfer made before any video call or visit to a seller who cannot be checked.
- **q05:** the take-back promise is in the written contract.
