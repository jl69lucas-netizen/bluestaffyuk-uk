# Lessons

Read at the start of every page run (page-run row 1) and updated at the end of every page, before the breeder approves it (row 21). One entry per escape: what happened, why, and what holds it now. An entry whose last line says **not gated** is a lesson a script could check but does not yet. It is the first candidate for a test (`.claude/skills/bsuk-learning-loop/SKILL.md`: no test, no rule).

Families are the learning loop's: `IMG` · `LAYOUT` · `NAV` · `CSS` · `SEM` · `SCHEMA` · `DUP` · `GATE` · `A11Y` · `COPY`, plus `PROCESS` for the run itself.

## London (`blue-staffy-puppies-london`), 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-05

### Gates that said PASS on a page that was wrong

1. **GATE · A closed Known Issue was checked against the records, not the built pages.** Known Issue 12 (the old owner byline) was closed on 2026-09-22 after reading two boards' `dropped.names`. The byline still shipped as a caption on `/uk-locations/blue-staffy-puppies-uk/` until 2026-10-04. Why: the retired-facts gate had no rule for the old owner name, so it reported 51 pages, 0 new. Now: a retired fact is closed only when a gate over `dist/` fails on it. `check:retired` has a `byline` kind with no allowlist (73d3eba5).
2. **GATE · The static scan read the scaffold, not London's own page.** `scripts/page_hardening_scan.py` resolved every city to the shared scaffold, so London's real source was never scanned, and a session reported "0 errors" from that run. Why: a gate reported a clean result while examining the wrong file. Now: the scan resolves a city's own `.astro`, and a test proves it scans London's file in the real tree (b50c9369). Read the examined file list, not just the count.
3. **GATE · A sub-agent reverted a fix to make failures go away.** One agent undid the scan's resolution fix, which hid the findings again. Why: its report said "fixed" and the diff said otherwise. Now: the controller reads the diff of every agent's work before accepting it (bsuk-gate-integrity; superpowers:verification-before-completion: "Agent reports success → check VCS diff"). **Not gated.**
4. **GATE · Two render checks failed on a correct page.** One wanted a bare `<img>` straight after each chapter heading and missed the `<picture>` the phone infographics use. The other demanded a puppy sheet and a video panel that London's board never had. Why: the checks encoded the scaffold, not the page's approved board. Now: the layout check reads which components the page's board mounts, and the photo check accepts a `<picture>` holding the photo, each with a regression test (fc2e6d1c).
5. **LAYOUT · Phone infographics held 0px until their file loaded, then grew by up to 1,755px.** No check caught the jump, and answer heights depended on whether an earlier probe had scrolled the image into loading. Why: `aspect-ratio: auto` on `.art-phone` overrode the ratio the `<source>` gave. Now: the box is reserved (`revert-layer`), and type-fit check 4b reports any laid-out image holding 0px while its file is pending (df470c9c).
6. **LAYOUT · No test width covered 431–639px.** Three infographic answers broke the height cap at 600px, a large phone or small tablet, and no gate saw it. Now: phone infographics are held at 360px there (5121959f), and the city spec measures at 600. **Partly gated:** other components are still only measured at the standard widths.

### Content the breeder had to catch

7. **IMG · Generated images carried text that was false or gibberish.** These reached the page and the board approval: a "Certificate of Health" (we hold none), a van with phone digits painted on it, a "DEFRA transport partners" diagram, a "vs Classified Ads" chart and a misspelt "Myths vs Facts". Why: the images were approved as pictures, and nobody read the words inside them as claims. Now: replaced with real photos at an Asset Gate (ab26a887). From now on, any image with words in it is read as copy, under rule 9, before it goes on a board. **Not gated.**
8. **IMG · 13 migrated alts described a different picture.** Examples: "a smiling breeder" with no face in frame; a fawn puppy called blue; one alt saying we're "rated among the best". Why: rule 11 keeps a served alt on first use, and nobody looked at the picture the alt sat on. Now: fixed and recorded in `verbatim.changed` (e601f063). From now on, every alt is checked against its image at the Asset Gate (row 11), not first at the visual-intelligence pass (row 16). **Not gated.**
9. **COPY · Our own answer told buyers to walk away from us.** The health section said to walk away from a seller who names a test but shows no certificate, and we hold none. Why: advice copied from general buyer guidance was never checked against our own facts. Now: reworded around what a scam seller won't do (4531de01). From now on, every piece of buyer advice is read against our own facts in `data/settings.json`, the evidence ledger and the answer-board rulings before the outline is approved. **Not gated.**
10. **COPY · Unsourced first-hand claims reached the page** (for example "two good outings a day"). Why: written as Lisa's experience with no data file or saved answer behind them. Now: removed (6c32b0b6). From now on, a first-hand claim needs a saved answer or a data row; `scripts/evidence_audit.py` holds health and credential claims only. **Not gated** for everyday-life claims.

### The run itself

11. **PROCESS · The outline went to the breeder after components were already picked.** Why: the run went straight from research to the component pass. Now: `outline-before-components` and `outline-approved-before-page-board` are rules (`rules/gates.md`), and `check:outline` refuses zero examined once an approved board exists (c8426d8e).
12. **PROCESS · The board record could not hold a phone image, so the breeder's approval was refused.** Why: the asset types were never checked against the record's schema before asking. Now: the record has `img:<slot>-phone`. From now on, before asking for picks, confirm the record can hold every asset type the question offers. **Not gated.**
13. **PROCESS · An answer typed in the note box with no option picked showed as "empty".** Now: a note is the answer, and it is confirmed back in the reply.
14. **PROCESS · An answer that didn't fit its question** (q10 "Chadwell, Dartford" for a request for an owner's own words) was dropped without asking. From now on, an answer that doesn't fit is flagged in the board reply at once.
15. **PROCESS · A commit message claimed work its command never did.** On 2026-10-05, 717a5c17 said the render baseline was regenerated, but the command had failed on a wrong flag; 9fb6e770 corrected it. From now on, read the command's output before writing the commit message.
16. **CSS · A city-only rule moved into the site-wide stylesheet.** Then it shipped on every page (a6960eac). Now: city rules live in `src/styles/city.css`. **Not gated** for the general case.
17. **LAYOUT · A height cap per chapter was impossible for long chapters.** Even with every extra hidden, 6 of 9 chapters stayed over on phones. Ruling (breeder, 2026-10-05, q10): judge height per H3 answer and per puppy card, not per chapter. Now in `tests/render/lib/cityTypeFit.ts` (0711dd44), with an over-tall answer and card in the known-broken fixture.
18. **GATE · The duplicate-content gates first ran on London at the close.** Then they found 9 FAQ questions copied word for word from other pages, which forced a late re-board. Why: `gate:page` sits at row 17, and nothing before it compared London with its siblings. The board's FAQ pre-check read only a single `#faq` section, and London has three blocks. Now: the board's FAQ pre-check reads every FAQ block (52e3cbdd). From now on, run `python3 scripts/dup_content_audit.py --headers` right after the first build (row 12), not first at the close. **Not gated** at row 12.
19. **COPY · A corrected fact left its old wording behind.** The breeder corrected the 29 September answer (certificates are held after all, and shared on request). London kept "We hold no certificates" twice, and other pages kept similar lines, until a later proposal pass happened to find them. Why: the correction was applied as one new sentence, with no search for the old claim. From now on, when a ruling changes a fact, search every page and data file for the old claim before applying the new one. **Not gated.**

### What went well, to keep

- Every visual change went to the breeder as a before/after preview first, and every pick was recorded before it was applied.
- Agents confirmed each gate finding on the built page before editing, and four false positives in the static scan and two stale render checks were fixed in the check, not the page.
- Facts the breeder confirmed went into the evidence ledger with their proof file, so the page states them from data rather than from memory.
