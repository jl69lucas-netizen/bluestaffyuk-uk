What Do London Buyers Ask Before Reserving a Puppy?

The first questions from London are nearly always about money and the journey, so here are our straight answers on the prices, the deposit, delivery and collection.

01

How Much Does a Blue Staffy Puppy Cost If I Live in London?

The price is the same wherever you live: £1,500 for each of our boys, Roman, Byrd and Ince, and £1,700 for each of our girls, Vennie, Christa and Cheryl.

02

Which Puppies Can I Ask About Right Now?

All six are available: Roman (blue and white), Byrd (white) and Ince (blue) are the boys, and Vennie (blue and white), Christa (blue) and Cheryl (blue with a white blaze) are the girls.

03

What Does the £500 Deposit Do?

Your £500 deposit books your viewing and reserves your puppy, and it comes off the price, so it is part of what you pay rather than an extra on top.

04

Do You Deliver Puppies to London?

Yes. We bring your puppy to your door in London by DEFRA-approved transport, and delivery costs £200–£350, priced by distance.

05

Can I Collect My Puppy From Carlisle Instead?

Yes. If you would rather make the trip yourself, you can collect your puppy from us in Carlisle instead of paying for home delivery.

06

How Do I Reserve a Puppy From London?

Send us the enquiry form with the name of the puppy you like, and we reply by email within 24 to 48 business hours to talk through the next steps.

How Can You Check Us From London Before You Travel?

You cannot pop round the corner from London, so we put the checks in your hands: the parents, their tests, our vet and what happens if something goes wrong.

07

Who Are the Parents of Your Puppies?

Maggie is our dam and Jones is our sire. Both are our own dogs, both are KC registered, and both were health tested before the litter.

08

Which Health Tests Do the Parents Have?

Maggie and Jones are both DNA tested clear of L-2-HGA and HC-HSF4, and both have had their eyes and elbows screened. Our health page explains what each test covers.

09

Can I Speak to Your Vet Before I Decide?

Yes. Buyers are welcome to contact our vet, so you can hear about the parents and the litter from someone other than us before you commit.

10

Is There a Health Guarantee With Every Puppy?

Yes. Every puppy we place comes with a two-year genetic health guarantee, and the cover is the same for a home in London as for one anywhere else.

11

What Happens If I Can No Longer Keep My Puppy?

We take the puppy back, and we do the same if a fault is ours. Tell us as soon as you know, and we will talk it through with you.

12

How Are Your Puppies Raised Before They Leave?

In our home, not in a kennel. Every litter is raised on Puppy Culture with Early Neurological Stimulation (ENS), and grows up among everyday household sights and sounds.

Will a Staffy Suit Your London Home?

London life means flats, busy parks and long days at work, so these answers cover space, exercise, children, time alone, training and how long a Staffy shares your home.

13

Can a Staffy Live Happily in a London Flat?

Yes, as long as the dog gets its daily exercise and something to think about. A Staffy is a medium-sized, people-focused dog, so a big garden matters less than regular walks.

14

How Much Exercise Does a Staffy Need Each Day?

At least an hour of vigorous exercise a day, ideally split into two sessions, plus play or training that works the mind as well as the legs.

15

Are Staffies Good With Children?

A well-socialised Staffordshire Bull Terrier is a patient family companion, which is where the old nickname, the nanny dog, comes from. Supervision still matters with any dog.

16

Can a Staffy Be Left Alone While I Am at Work?

Not for long stretches. Staffies are people-focused dogs that want company, so a puppy needs to be built up to short spells alone, a little at a time.

17

Are Staffies Easy to Train?

They learn quickly and they are strong-willed with it, so short, consistent sessions with rewards work far better than long ones or a raised voice.

18

Is a Staffy a Good First Dog?

Yes, for a household ready to socialise the puppy early and train it consistently. Staffies are eager to please, but they need company, so the commitment matters most.

19

How Long Does a Staffordshire Bull Terrier Live?

Twelve to fourteen years is the figure the Staffordshire Bull Terrier Club gives for a healthy, well-cared-for dog. Diet, exercise and regular vet checks decide where in that range a dog lands.
