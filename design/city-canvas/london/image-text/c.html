Where Does Your Puppy Start, and How Does It Reach London?

In our home in Carlisle, Cumbria, with its mother close by, and then at your door in London or in your arms at collection, whichever suits you.

1Carlisle

Who Raises Your Puppy Before It Leaves Carlisle?

We do, in our own home, with Maggie beside her puppies. Every litter is raised with Puppy Culture and ENS, and both parents, Maggie and Jones, are DNA-tested clear of L-2-HGA and HC-HSF4 with their eyes and elbows screened.

2London

How Does Your Puppy Get From Us to Your Door?

By DEFRA-approved transport, for £200 to £350 priced by distance, or you collect from us in Carlisle. The £500 deposit books your viewing and reserves your puppy, and it comes off the price.
