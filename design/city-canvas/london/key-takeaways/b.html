What Do You Need to Know Before Booking a Viewing?

Four things to settle before you pay anything, whether you live in Hackney or Harrow, each with a plain answer from Lisa Bright in Carlisle.

  • The price on the card is the price: £1,500 for a boy, £1,700 for a girl.
  • A £500 deposit books your viewing and reserves your puppy, and it comes off the price.
  • London delivery is £200 to £350 by DEFRA-approved transport, priced by distance, or you collect in Carlisle.
  • Both parents are DNA-tested for L-2-HGA and HC-HSF4, and every puppy has a two-year genetic health guarantee.
